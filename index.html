HOW TO PLAY

YOUR FIRST EXPEDITION

Talk to the Quartermaster in Sanctuary to begin The Last Signal. Explore nearby sectors, gather materials, and return to Sanctuary to deposit materials in your vault and bank salvage. Farther sectors bring stronger enemies and new hazards.

Use the tactical map to track discoveries, and check the biome effects panel when you enter a new region. Environmental hazards can hurt you even when no enemies are nearby.

CONTROLS

Open Settings from the main menu or Pause to rebind keyboard and mouse controls, reduce visual effects, or customize the crosshair.

QUESTS AND BEACONS

Follow the quest HUD and cyan map diamonds for field objectives. Stay near a signal mast while it scans and inside the marked perimeter during a defense. You can have three active quests, including the main quest. NPC side quests, repeatable Armourer contracts, and the craftable Bounty Board are optional.

The campaign supplies a kit for one beacon repair. Other stations require materials. Repaired beacons protect you from local biome hazards and let you fast-travel between repaired stations and Sanctuary from the tactical map.

OPTIONAL WORLD EVENTS

After five minutes of active overworld play, watch for distress signals, supply wrecks, moving horde trails, disabled convoys, and Void breaches (sector 3 onward). Approach to discover an amber map marker, then use your interact binding at the site to opt in. Events have their own tracker and do not use quest slots.

Clear three cargo-lock ambushes, defend a survivor for 90 seconds, repair a convoy with parts on site and defend it for 60 seconds, intercept a horde leader and escort, or shoot three breach anchors before fighting its guardian. Watch reinforcement warnings. Completion pays sector-scaled salvage and supplies; an ignored event expires after eight active minutes.

Use Abandon in the event tracker or leave its marked area to withdraw without a completion reward. Ignoring an event never damages Sanctuary, beacons, or campaign progress. Event timers freeze while paused, reading the map or journal, managing stations, inside the facility, and during live story scans or defenses.

DISCOVERY JOURNAL

Open Journal beside the quest log or from Pause. Reading pauses the game. Encounters reveal enemy identities and abilities; your first defeat unlocks combat advice, and five defeats complete a standard variant's entry. Boss entries complete after one victory. Discoveries and recovered story records stay in their save slot, with undiscovered enemies and story details hidden.

WEAPONS AND AMMO

Use your weapon-slot bindings or the weapon bar to switch weapons. Each weapon keeps its magazine when you switch away. The ammo reserve counts full reloads, not individual rounds. Melee weapons use no ammo. Check your reserves and bring medkits before a long expedition.

Earn permanent Armourer reputation through contracts and spend salvage on weapon and armor blueprints. Some blueprints come from encounters instead. Unlocking a blueprint lets you craft the gear; it does not equip it.

SANCTUARY REBUILDING AND WEAPON MODS

Talk to the Medic, Mechanic, Scout, Archivist, and Quartermaster to rebuild five Sanctuary projects using deposited materials. Each has two visible stages; stage II requires exploring sector 3. Existing NPC quests and services stay available. Improvements belong to the current save, not every run.

The Mechanic's Workshop unlocks a Handling slot at stage I and a Specialization slot at stage II. Craft a compatible mod once per weapon, then install or remove it free at the Mechanic. Every mod shows its trade-off. Advanced plans come from deterministic Archivist research: enemy encounters, player defeats, and completed world events, never random drops or free weapon unlocks.

Quick Loader reduces magazine size and empty-magazine recovery. Removed rounds become shared loose ammo, shown as R beside full reload reserves, and are used first when reloading. Swapping mods never fills a magazine. Medical Wing II reduces medkit material requirements by 15%, rounded down with a minimum of one.

The Scout adds approximate map leads in explored regions without discovering or activating events. The Archive displays recovered records and earned trophies. The Supply Depot crafts equipped-weapon ammo and, at stage II, a saved multi-weapon order with a full cost preview. Orders use normal recipe costs and crafting time; supplies are never free. Optional mini-facilities and named-rival dossiers arrive in later expansion stages.

AFTER THE CAMPAIGN

Check the quest log for the Continuity Key clue after the final turn-in. The portal is northeast, 10,000 meters beyond the map edge. The map shows a search area, not the exact portal. Bring supplies, avoid the marked Void storm strikes and unstable rifts, and defeat the three-phase Void Stalker to enter the facility. Stock up before entering the Foreman's arena, and watch for floor markings and purple reinforcement gates.

SAVES AND BACKUPS

Save to one of five slots from Pause and resume a slot from the main menu. Export Current Run backs up even unsaved progress. In Load Save, export individual slots, back up all slots, or preview a .hordesave import before confirming a destination.

Dungeon saves resume at the latest safe checkpoint with your saved health and supplies, not in the middle of a boss phase. Death still ends the run.

CHANGE LOG

LATEST UPDATE: THE LAST SIGNAL

  • Sanctuary rebuilding: Five projects with two visible stages, NPC services, medkit savings, Scout signal leads, Archivist research and trophy displays, and paid multi-weapon resupply orders.
  • Weapon modifications: Two slots per weapon, 13 compatible handling and specialization options with explicit trade-offs, deterministic research plans, free installation at the Mechanic, and ammo-conserving magazine changes. All progression is per save.
  • World events: Five optional encounters bring survivor defenses, cargo ambushes, roaming horde interceptions, convoy repairs, and Void breaches to the frontier. Discover map markers, opt in at the site, and earn sector-scaled completion rewards. Event progress and journal records are saved; ignoring an event has no permanent penalty.
  • Discovery journal: A paused field guide with enemy research, discovered regions and facility rooms, Void hazard notes, and recovered campaign records. Open it beside the quest log, from Pause, or with its remappable shortcut. Progress stays with each save.
  • Deep Void expedition: The portal now lies 10,000 meters beyond the map edge. New storm strikes and rift ambushes threaten the journey. The Void Stalker has 1,400 HP, faster pursuit, and three phases featuring lunges, radial volleys, spread shots, rift marks, crossing lanes, and shockwaves. Phase two chains two attacks; phase three chains three before a recovery opening. Every follow-up has its own warning, including when summoned by the Foreman.
  • Campaign: 12 missions across four chapters, with signal tracking, beacon defense, and a final expedition. Existing saves get credit for equivalent milestones.
  • Optional progression: Sanctuary NPC quests, a craftable Bounty Board with escalating contracts, and Armourer reputation and blueprints.
  • Void epilogue: The Continuity Key leads to a hidden portal guarded by the redesigned Void Stalker, with telegraphed lunges and recovery windows. Beyond it are eight rooms, a relay puzzle, optional caches, and safe checkpoints.
  • The Foreman: A tougher three-phase fight with new attacks and escalating hordes drawn from every biome family and the Void. The Void Stalker is the rarest summon. First victory unlocks the Continuity Weave blueprint; rematches remain available.
  • Menus and settings: Refreshed Pause menu, remappable keyboard and mouse controls, Reduced Effects, and crosshair customization.
  • Saving: Verified browser saves with IndexedDB fallback, cross-tab protection, and portable .hordesave backups with import previews.
  • Combat fixes: Projectile hits check the full path traveled between frames, Hazmat blocks radioactive damage and blinding, and automated beacon kills no longer count toward player kill quests. Minigun ammo crafting makes one belt at the same material cost.
